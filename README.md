### ABOUT ME

Security Analyst at **Qubit Capital** with 2+ years across threat intelligence, DFIR, and SOC operations. I investigate phishing and BEC at scale — 250+ incidents across 1,300+ domains and 2,700+ inboxes — and automate the toil away with n8n SOAR pipelines that cut response time from 4 hours to 75 minutes. M3AAWG member, previously infra + email security at UnifyCX.

### FOCUS

- **Email security** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC at scale, BEC, phishing forensics, lookalike-domain takedowns
- **Threat intelligence** — IOC enrichment, campaign tracking, adversary profiling, finished intel
- **Detection engineering** — YARA, Sigma, STIX/TAXII, MITRE ATT&CK mapping
- **AI security** — AI threat research, agentic threat modeling, prompt-injection defense, non-human identity
